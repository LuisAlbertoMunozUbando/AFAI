The opportunity

Robotics moves fast.
Engineering knowledge gets left behind.

Automation teams repeatedly research technologies, compare components, and troubleshoot challenges that other engineers have already encountered. The answers exist — but they are scattered across documents, videos, vendors and individual experts.

01 / FRAGMENTATION

Knowledge is everywhere

Critical lessons live in project reports, demonstration videos, vendor documentation and people's heads.

02 / REWORK

Teams start from scratch

Previous decisions, integration failures and validated approaches are difficult to discover and compare.

03 / DEPLOYMENT

Breakthroughs stay in the lab

Moving from an impressive robotics demonstration to reliable industrial deployment requires practical engineering context.

Our approach

From scattered experience to reusable intelligence.

Automation Foundry is being developed as an AI-powered knowledge and integration layer for industrial robotics teams — starting with searchable, structured automation cases.

“Not another robot. The intelligence layer that helps engineers deploy robots.”
01
Capture engineering evidence

Extract useful information from technical videos, reports, vendor manuals and project records.

02
Structure automation cases

Organise problems, constraints, hardware, methods, failures, costs and lessons learnt.

03
Ask engineering questions

Retrieve comparable implementations and compare options with traceable supporting evidence.

04
Accelerate integration

Reuse knowledge to plan, validate and eventually assemble higher-level robotics capabilities.

Designed for Physical AI

A knowledge layer for the robotics ecosystem.

Long-term vision

Engineering innovation should scale in parallel.

Just as GPU computing allows thousands of operations to run in parallel, we envision an engineering ecosystem where teams build on reusable knowledge and validated robotics capabilities rather than repeating work sequentially.

Our ambition is to become the collective engineering memory and integration backbone for Physical AI — from factories and warehouses to agriculture and beyond.
