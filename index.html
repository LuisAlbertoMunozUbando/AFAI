AFAI NEXT · PROPOSED ROADMAP

Agentic Industrial Collaboration & Negotiation

From a Physical AI Engineering Copilot to an agentic engineering coordination platform: creative, industrial and hardware agents collaborate, negotiate constraints, and coordinate human-approved engineering execution. These capabilities are a product vision, not yet deployed features.

Innovation Agents

Research, technical ideas, prior art, design concepts and prototyping.

Negotiation Agents

Supplier proposals, budgets, cost/quality/time trade-offs and documented agreements.

Manufacturing Agents

CNC, additive manufacturing, winding, PCBA, assembly, sourcing and quality.

Physical AI Agents

Simulation, ROS 2, perception, Jetson, robot planning and supervised execution.

Governance & IP Agents

Provenance, confidentiality, patent considerations, audit and safety approvals.

Business & Investment Agents

Customer discovery, unit economics, licensing, startup and partnership opportunities.

Agent-to-Agent Negotiation

Capability discovery → Contract Net requests for proposals → multi-objective negotiation → coalition formation → verifiable agreements → approved execution. Example: agents jointly optimize a low-cost robotic actuator across motor winding, gearbox machining, PCB, thermal constraints, suppliers and final price.

Connect existing IdeaFactory tools

YouTube2Knowledge learns industrial procedures; VisionFromYouTube analyzes visual operations; Knowledge provides shared evidence and memory; STL monitors science, technology and patents; Innovaction supports concept development. Proposed orchestration uses agent-to-agent communication, MCP tools, structured engineering cases, CAD/PLM/ERP adapters and industrial integration.

Proposed 90-day pilot

Days 1–30: designer, manufacturing, procurement and negotiation agents produce a traceable actuator proposal. Days 31–60: connect AFAI knowledge tools, suppliers and multi-objective evaluation. Days 61–90: add simulation and a supervised physical demonstration; measure engineering time, proposal quality and cost.

Commercial pathways

Engineering Copilot subscriptions; a future B2B Negotiation Network; and enterprise Physical Execution integrations. Agentic Industrial Economics is a proposed research theme on resource allocation, industrial bargaining and value creation by AI agents.

Safety and trust: no autonomous binding contracts, spending, IP disclosure or physical machine commands without explicit authorization, independent safety controls, and qualified human verification. Confidential engineering data requires appropriate agreements and access controls.
